THE BASICS

HOW TO PLAY

01 / MOVE. Move your mouse over the game field, or touch and drag your finger on a phone or tablet. A visible white cursor ring follows you.

02 / COLLECT. Intercept the moving colored dots. Each one you touch adds to your score and combo. No clicks or keyboard timing.

03 / KEEP UP. Faster patterns appear as the original phonk beat progresses. Missed dots reset your combo. Every completed run earns coins for cosmetic note skins.

04 / PAUSE. Press Space or the Pause button. Sound starts only after you choose Start, as required by browsers.
